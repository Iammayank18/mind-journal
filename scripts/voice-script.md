# Mind Journal — meditation voice script

19 batches · 168 lines. Generate each batch as **one** recording.

## How to record (Google AI Studio → Generate speech)

1. Open **aistudio.google.com → Generate speech** (single speaker).
2. Pick a soft voice and keep it the same for every batch. Good ones to try: **Achernar** (soft),
   **Vindemiatrix** (gentle), **Sulafat** (warm), **Aoede** (breezy); for a male voice, **Enceladus** (breathy).
3. Paste the **style instructions** below into the style / instructions box (or at the top of the text).
4. Paste one batch's lines as the text, generate, listen, and download.
5. Save the file in `scripts/voice-batches/` with the batch's name — e.g. `01-learn-1.wav` (wav or mp3).
6. When all are saved: `node scripts/voice-batches.mjs import --voice gemini-<VoiceName>`

If a batch reads a line twice, skips one, or doesn't leave clear pauses, just regenerate that batch —
the import tells you which ones need it.

## Style instructions (same for every batch)

```
Read these lines as an experienced, kind meditation teacher guiding one person.
Voice: warm and grounded, from the chest — a natural, slightly low pitch for this voice. Soft but clearly audible, not whispered.
Tone: gentle and reassuring, with a faint smile in the voice. Calm, never cheerful, dramatic or salesy. No vocal fry.
Pace: slow and even — about 100 to 120 words per minute. Let sentences settle downward at the end, not rise.
Breathe naturally between phrases; pause briefly at commas and dashes, longer after each sentence.
Keep the volume steady from start to finish.
After each numbered line, stay completely silent for about 3 seconds before the next one.
Do not read the line numbers. Do not add, skip or repeat any words.
```

## 01-learn-1  ·  Learn to meditate — Arriving  ·  14 lines

Save as: `scripts/voice-batches/01-learn-1.wav`

```
1. Welcome. Find a position that feels steady and comfortable — sitting or lying down.

2. Let your eyes close, or rest your gaze softly on the floor.

3. Take one deeper breath in… and a long, slow breath out.

4. Now let the breath find its own rhythm. You don't need to control it.

5. Notice where you feel the breath most clearly. The nose, the chest, or the belly.

6. Just rest your attention there, on the feeling of breathing.

7. Slowly, begin to let the practice go. Let the breath be completely natural.

8. Notice how you feel right now. There's nothing to change.

9. In a moment you'll hear the bell. Take your time coming back.

10. If your mind has wandered, that's completely normal. Gently come back to the breath.

11. Each time you notice and return — that is the practice.

12. Breathing in, knowing you're breathing in. Breathing out, knowing you're breathing out.

13. Let the body be heavy and supported.

14. No need to chase calm. Just keep the breath company.
```

## 02-learn-2  ·  Learn to meditate — Counting breaths  ·  9 lines

Save as: `scripts/voice-batches/02-learn-2.wav`

```
1. Settle in. Let your shoulders drop and your jaw soften.

2. A few slow breaths to arrive. In… and out.

3. Now start counting each out-breath. One… two… three… up to ten.

4. When you reach ten, start again from one. If you lose count, simply start again. No problem at all.

5. Counting each breath out, quietly, in your mind.

6. Lost count? That's okay. Begin again at one.

7. Let the numbers be soft, almost a whisper.

8. Notice the little pause at the end of each out-breath.

9. If you're past ten, smile, and return to one.
```

```

7. Your chest and upper back, rising and falling with the breath.

8. Your belly. Let it be soft.

9. Your lower back, your hips, the places you're touching the floor or chair.

10. Down through your legs, to your knees, your calves.

11. Your feet. The soles of your feet, your toes.

12. Now feel the whole body at once, breathing.
```

## 12-sleep-2  ·  Sleep wind-down — Counting down  ·  9 lines

Save as: `scripts/voice-batches/12-sleep-2.wav`

```
1. Lie comfortably and close your eyes.

2. With each out-breath, count down silently, starting at fifty.

3. Fifty… forty-nine… forty-eight… If you lose your place, start again anywhere.

4. Counting down, one number for each slow breath out.

5. Let each number feel heavier and slower than the last.

6. Lost the count? Begin again. It doesn't matter at all.

7. Sinking a little deeper with every number.

8. Let the numbers fade away now.

9. Rest.
```
